# Domain-Driven Design Deliverables

## Discovery
- Business problem statement.
- Stakeholder and expert map.
- Domain glossary.
- Business capability inventory.
- Known rules, exceptions, and pain points.

## Strategic design
- Subdomain classification: core, supporting, generic.
- Candidate bounded contexts.
- Context map with relationship direction and integration style.
- Ownership and language boundaries.

## Tactical design
- Aggregate boundaries.
- Invariants and transaction boundaries.
- Entities and value objects.
- Domain services and domain events.
- Repository responsibilities.

## Architecture and operations
- Integration contracts.
- Event schema and compatibility policy.
- Idempotency and retry strategy.
- Observability and reconciliation.
- Migration and rollback plan.

Produce only artifacts that answer a real design question. A large document set is not evidence of good DDD.
